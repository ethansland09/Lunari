HOW LUNARI WORKS

1. Write the scrambled Lunari letters.
2. Turn the writing upside down.
3. Point the camera at it.
4. Lunari automatically rotates the image 180°.
5. OCR reads the normal letters.
6. The cipher converts them back into English.

For best recognition, use dark writing on white paper, large letters, good lighting, and spaces between words.
